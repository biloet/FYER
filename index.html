How it works

Three steps. Zero traces.

Everything happens on Robinhood Chain. No bridges to a separate privacy chain, no new wallet to learn.

01

Shield

Deposit ETH or any supported token into the FYER pool. You receive a private note that only your key can spend.

02

Send & trade

Move value inside the shielded pool. A zk-proof shows the transaction is valid without revealing who, what or how much.

03

Withdraw

Unshield to any fresh address whenever you want. On-chain, there is no link between the deposit and the withdrawal.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is FYER?

FYER is a privacy layer for Robinhood Chain. It lets you shield assets into a zero-knowledge pool and then transact privately on the same chain.

Is FYER custodial?

No. Funds sit in an on-chain contract and can only be spent with proofs generated from your own keys. Nobody — including the FYER team — can move them.

Which wallets are supported?

Any EVM wallet that injects window.ethereum: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Robinhood Wallet and more.

What does it cost?

0.3% to shield, 0% for private transfers, 0.3% to withdraw — plus normal Robinhood Chain gas.

How do I get ETH on Robinhood Chain?

Bridge ETH from Ethereum or another supported network using the official Robinhood Chain bridge, then shield it with FYER.
